Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Spendly collects information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the expense data you log. We may also collect basic usage information to help improve the Service.

Your data is used solely to operate and improve Spendly. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and sending account-related notifications. We do not use your financial data for advertising or any other commercial purpose.

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website. We will respond to your inquiry as promptly as possible.
